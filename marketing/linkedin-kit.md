## 6. Two-week cadence

Consistency beats volume. ~30 min/day:

- **Day 1:** Profile overhaul (sections 1–3). Launch post (section 4).
- **Days 2–5:** 5 warm reconnects/day from your existing connections
  (State Farm, National General, Smart Financial, Charter, Hanon McKendry
  alumni). Warm beats cold ~10:1.
- **Days 6–10:** 5 targeted cold DMs/day, one lane per day (agencies Mon,
  small biz Tue, publishers Wed...). Comment substantively on 3 posts/day in
  insurance + web dev feeds — comments are the discovery engine.
- **Day 11:** Second post: a build-in-public breakdown ("How I shipped a
  61-claim platform with zero frameworks") ending with the /hire link.
- **Days 12–14:** Follow up (once, only once) with anyone who engaged.
- **Every scope call booked:** ask "who else should I be talking to?" —
  referrals compound.

Track it: a simple sheet — date, name, lane, template used, reply?, call?,
quote?, closed $. What gets measured gets closed.
